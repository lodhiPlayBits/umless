Billing questions

What counts as a take?

One recording you transcribe. Recording again without transcribing doesn't count.

Can I cancel any time?

Yes. You keep Pro until the end of the period you've paid for, then move to Free.

Do unused takes roll over?

No. Free takes reset on the first of each month.
