FAQ

Questions, answered

Which filler words does umless catch?

um, uh and mhmm. "Uh-huh" and "uh-uh" mean yes and no, so they count as ordinary words.

What do the dots in my transcript mean?

Each dot is one second of silence. A pause of three seconds shows as three dots.

Is my recording stored?

Not on our server. Your audio is sent to our speech-to-text provider to make the transcript. Keep a copy by downloading it.

Which browsers work?

Current Chrome and Edge are fully tested. Firefox works, and Safari is best-effort for now.

How long can an answer be?

Up to 10 minutes. You'll see a countdown in the last minute.

Which languages are supported?

English for now.
